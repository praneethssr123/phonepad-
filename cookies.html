Version 1.0 · Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies and similar browser storage (such as local storage) are small pieces of data stored on your device by your browser.

2. What this website uses

Name Type Purpose Duration
phonepad.consent.v1 Essential (local storage) Remembers your cookie choices so we can honour them on every visit. Until you clear it

3. Consent and control

  • On your first visit you can Accept All, Reject Non-Essential, or save custom preferences.
  • No non-essential cookies are set or read before valid consent.
  • You can revisit and change your choice at any time via Cookie Preferences (also linked in the footer of every page).
  • Withdrawing consent stops new non-essential cookies from being set.

4. Do Not Track

We honour your explicit consent choice; your browser's Do Not Track signal is treated as "Reject Non-Essential" until you make an active choice.
